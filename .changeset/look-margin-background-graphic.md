The «Utseende» card of the admin tool sets three more things: the margin above and below the list and the form, a background color behind them, and a graphic behind the heading (a pattern, or an image from Content Studio) with a slider for how much of it shows. The slider also applies to an app's own decoration behind the heading, which is now in a box of its own. `DesignDefaults` takes `background`, `margin` and `graphicOpacity`, all optional.
